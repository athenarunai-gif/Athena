# Step 5 — outreach and community

## Best-of list emails

Most "best of" lists that AI answers cite have a contact page or a named author. One
accepted edit can keep AthenaRun inside a cited answer for months.

Draft, don't send. One file per target: `seo/outbox/<date>-<domain>.md` with the
target URL, the contact route you found, and the draft below.

```
Subject: <their list title> — one category you're missing

Hi <name>,

I read <exact list title> while researching <the buying question>. It's the piece
that keeps coming up.

One gap: every tool on it is a builder — you assemble the app. There's a newer
category where you write the goal and the platform generates the whole application,
database and permissions included. AthenaRun (Frankfurt, GDPR, self-hostable) does
that; an HR team ships an onboarding portal without opening a ticket with IT.

If it fits, here's the page: <URL>. Happy to send a 2-minute recording of it
building something live instead, if that's more useful than a link.

<name>
AthenaRun · athenarun.com
```

Rules: no attachment, no deck, no follow-up sequence, one email per person. Say what
their list is missing, not what we sell. If we don't genuinely belong on the list,
skip it — a bad pitch costs the next one too.

## Community answers

Answer the real question first. The link, if any, goes at the end and only when it
actually answers what was asked.

Where our buyers ask: LinkedIn (HR ops, RevOps, compliance communities), YouTube
comments under the videos AI already cites for our keywords, Quora, r/nocode,
r/msp, r/humanresources, German forums and XING groups.

**Re-verify each week which surfaces are cited.** Citation sources move — a platform
that was heavily cited last quarter can be dropped from AI answers entirely. Run the
buying question through search and look at what the answer actually cites this week
rather than trusting this list.

Draft answers into `seo/outbox/<date>-community.md` with the thread URL, the question
verbatim, and the draft reply. A human posts them — from a real account, in their own
voice.
