# Seed keyword families — AthenaRun

Buying intent only. A learning keyword ("what is low-code") is only worth writing as
part of a pillar, never as one of the three weekly buying posts.

## Category / pillar candidates

- internal tools without developers
- AI app builder for business teams
- autonomous software creation
- build internal business apps without IT
- low-code alternative for enterprise teams

## Buying patterns × our buyers

Fill `<buyer>` with: HR teams, operations teams, compliance teams, finance teams,
small businesses, enterprises, non-technical teams, German companies.

- best internal tool builder for `<buyer>`
- best AI app builder for `<buyer>`
- best no-code platform for `<buyer>` 2026
- top 10 internal tools platforms
- `<competitor>` alternative / alternatives to `<competitor>`
- internal tools platform pricing (⚠ needs a real published price — see SKILL.md)
- cheapest way to build internal business apps
- best GDPR compliant no-code platform
- best self-hosted internal tools platform
- best HR onboarding software without IT
- best approval workflow software for operations teams
- best audit log / compliance dashboard builder

## Competitor set (for `<competitor> alternative` keywords)

Retool, Bubble, Airtable, Microsoft Power Apps, Glide, Softr, Appsmith, Budibase,
ServiceNow App Engine, Lovable, Replit, Zapier Interfaces, Make.

Write these honestly: state what the competitor is genuinely good at, then the
specific case where goal-in/app-out beats assembling components. A comparison post
that pretends the competitor is bad reads as marketing and does not get cited.

## German-language track

The GmbH, Frankfurt HQ, GDPR and self-hosting are a real moat in DACH, and the
German SERP is far less contested. Alternate: run the German set every other week.

- interne Tools ohne Entwickler
- Low-Code Plattform DSGVO-konform
- Power Apps Alternative Deutschland
- HR Software Onboarding ohne IT
- Business Apps selbst erstellen Vergleich

Publish German pages under `content/de/` with `hreflang` back to the English page.

## Rules

- One keyword per post. If a post could rank for two searches, it will be quoted
  for neither.
- Check the keyword is a question a person with a budget types, not a definition.
- Skip a keyword where the honest answer is "AthenaRun isn't the right tool" —
  those posts convert nobody and cost trust.
